### 1.2 Results and analysis

**It has not been sent, and that is the honest state of this task.** The owner was messaged once, about DK-01. Ten PRs later he has not been told the rest exists. A message covering the blockers is drafted and held outside this repository; sending it is Dingyi's to do and has not been done.

**What the approval pack adds over the handoff.** The handoff is organised by artifact — here is the candidate, here are the blockers, here is where everything lives. That is the wrong shape for a person who has to answer. The approval pack is organised **by reviewer**: Chengshuai Yang has 21 items, Ting Xue has 9, Dingyi has 6 of which 3 are settled. Nobody has to work out which of eleven task records applies to them.

**"Regenerate checks/hashes if files change" is already cheap.** Any approved correction changes the PDF and invalidates the recorded hashes. Re-running `verify_candidate.py` re-derives all six checks in one command, and DK-09 has demonstrated each of them fires on a real defect. So an approved change costs a rebuild, not a re-audit — which is what makes it safe to send the candidate before every correction is settled.
