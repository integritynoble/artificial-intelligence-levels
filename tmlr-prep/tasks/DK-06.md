## 2. The defect

`datasets/ai-level-bench-v0.4/verifiers/harmonized_scoring.py::delegation_frontier` returns the highest individually passing T band:

```python
passing=[t for (t,h),q in cell_scores.items() if h<=hi and q>=p]
return max(passing) if passing else None
```

The required rule is the highest T band such that **every lower band also passes** at the same H ceiling and p:
- Paper p. 18, note after Eq. (8): "the frontier is taken cumulatively — the largest T such that every band up to T meets p"
- Paper p. 23–24, §11.4: gate `z_DI,(b,h) = Δ·V·¬FC·L_h·K_T,<b|h`; "the frontier is the largest b for which that holds at h"
- Paper p. 69, addendum §5: `DF(h,p)=max{T_k : min_{j<=k} S_net(A,T_j,H<=h) >= p}`
- `cumulative_policy.json` → `objects.DI.frontier_law`: same formula

Example at H1, p = 0.80, S_net = {T0: .95, T1: .90, T2: .50, T3: .85}: helper returns **T3**, rule gives **T1** (pinned by tests).
