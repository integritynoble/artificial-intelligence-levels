## Sub-task 1 — Template and preservation

### 1.1 How we interpret it

| Phrase | Our interpretation |
|---|---|
| "the current official TMLR LaTeX template" | The style files TMLR itself publishes (`tmlr.sty`, `tmlr.bst`), used as the template intends: anonymous by default, `[preprint]` for a named build, `[accepted]` only at camera-ready. Not a look-alike. |
| "preserve the title, framework, definitions, equations and supported results" | The body is copied **unchanged**. Nothing is rewritten, summarized or dropped to fit. Preservation is something to demonstrate, not assert, so we verify it by comparing the built text. |
| "log substantive changes" | Every change is logged, with its kind: required by the template, layout repair, or substantive. A substantive change would need owner approval; this conversion makes none. |
| "do not impose an invented 12-page cap" | Length is whatever the content needs. No section is moved to an appendix or cut to hit a number. |
| Not part of this sub-task | Applying the wording proposals from DK-04/DK-06, or adding Dingyi to the author record (DK-02). |

### 1.2 What we did

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Read `tmlr.sty` to establish what the template supplies (geometry, fonts, `natbib`, the running head, the anonymization machinery) and therefore what the source must stop loading. |
| 2 | Wrote `convert_to_tmlr.py` so the conversion is reproducible and every rewrite prints a log line, instead of hand-editing 2,134 lines. |
| 3 | Generated a shared `paper_common.tex` plus two thin wrappers: `tmlr_submission.tex` (anonymous) and `tmlr_named.tex` (`[preprint]`). Both build from the same body, so the two editions cannot drift apart. |
| 4 | Built each edition: `pdflatex`, `bibtex`, then three more `pdflatex` passes. |
| 5 | Verified preservation by extracting the text of both the source build and the TMLR build and comparing word frequencies, which is robust to the table reflow that defeats a line-by-line diff. |
| 6 | Scanned the anonymous PDF for identity leaks: author names in the text, and the PDF metadata fields. |

### 1.3 Results and analysis

| Check | Anonymous build | Named build |
|---|---|---|
| Pages | 63 | 63 |
| Errors | 0 | 0 |
| Undefined references / citations | 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 |
| Overfull boxes | 0 | 0 |
| LaTeX warnings | 0 | 0 |
| PDF `Author` metadata | *(empty)* | Chengshuai Yang and Ting Xue and Dingyi Kang |
| Title block | "Anonymous authors / Paper under double-blind review" | three authors with affiliations |

**Preservation.** Source build 25,700 words against TMLR build 25,778 (+78). Every difference traces to the citation style: TMLR's author-year style prints "et al." and author surnames in the running text where `plainnat` printed bracketed numbers, and the reference list uses "pp." A handful of further differences are extraction artifacts of the font change (for example `$C^{\mathrm{GUI}}$` splitting differently). No body sentence differs.

**Analysis.**
1. **The conversion is mechanical, and that is the point.** Because the body is copied byte for byte and only the preamble and title block are rewritten, "preserve the content" is checkable rather than promised: any future re-run of the script reproduces the same edition from the same source.
2. **The citation style change is required, visible, and not substantive.** Numeric `[1]` becomes `(Amershi et al., 2019)`. No reference is added, dropped or altered; only its rendering changes. Logged as required.
3. **One anonymization defect existed and would not have been visible on the page.** The source's `\hypersetup` writes the authors into PDF metadata. The anonymous build now carries an empty `Author` field. The two author-looking names remaining in the text are third-party citations — John Yang of SWE-bench and Xueguang Ma of MMLU-Pro — not our authors.
4. **Length: 63 pages, and no cap was imposed.** Shorter than v2.6's 69 only because the four addendum pages are in the body now, the type block differs, and the float parameters were retuned. Nothing was moved or cut.
5. **Two identifiers remain for a decision**, not a fix: the words `hilbench` and `ailevel` in the naming note are searchable project names (DK-02/DK-07).

## Sub-task 2 — Repairs

### 2.1 How we interpret it

| Phrase | Our interpretation |
|---|---|
| "repair layout" | No overfull boxes, no text pushed outside the type block, no unreadable table. Cosmetic float gaps are worth noting but are not defects. |
| "cross-references" | Every `\ref`, `\eqref` and `\cite` resolves, **and points at the right thing**. A reference that resolves to the wrong number is still broken — including one baked into a figure image. |
| "bibliography" | Builds under TMLR's own style with no undefined citations and no missing entries. |
| "figure/table placement" | Every float appears, near its discussion, without splitting a table across a page break. |
| "owner approval for substantive relocation or removal" | Nothing is moved or removed here, so no approval is required for this sub-task. |

### 2.2 What we did

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Built, then read the log for overfull and underfull boxes, undefined references and undefined citations. |
| 2 | Inspected the rendered pages for the risky cases: the title block, the widest tables (Tables 19 and 20), the typed-cumulative table (17), both figure pages and the references. |
| 3 | Fixed the one overfull box: the §11.6 U-gate display, reset on two aligned lines. |
| 4 | Fixed the title line breaks, which wrapped with "Level:" alone on a line in the narrower TMLR title block. |
| 5 | Checked the equation numbers the figures cite, and repaired Figure 2's panel titles. |
| 6 | Measured float placement: counted pages with a vertical gap wider than 90pt, and pages below 35% text coverage. |
| 8 | On a third pass: inspected **every page** of the built PDF rather than a sample; checked font embedding; and compared the U-gate equation's rendering against the source build and the published PDF. |
| 7 | On a second pass: checked every table-of-contents page number against the page each heading starts on; listed every link annotation, external and internal; measured text blocks against the type block; and compared the front matter (date, keywords) with the source build. |

### 2.3 Results and analysis

| Repair | Detail |
|---|---|
| Overfull box | 1 → 0. The U-gate display overflowed by 14.9pt; now two aligned lines, same symbols and values |
| Title | Hand-set line breaks; the title block now reads as three balanced lines plus a three-line subtitle |
| Cross-reference in a figure | Figure 2's panels said "Eq. (9)" and "Eq. (10)". Those are the U-level gates; the primitives are (15) and (17). **The numbers are dropped, not corrected**, so they cannot go stale again when the manuscript is renumbered |
| Bibliography | Builds with `tmlr.bst`; 30 references, 0 undefined citations, 0 bibtex warnings |
| Underfull boxes | 18 (anonymous) / 19 (named). Cosmetic stretched lines; no text is lost or overset |
| Float placement | Two rounds. Retuning the parameters removed the one table-only page. Then a visual pass over every page found tables drifting **above their own headings** — §6.1, §11.2 and §15.2 each showed a heading with the next heading directly beneath it — so floats are now confined to their section, subsection and subsubsection. Correct placement costs about three pages and leaves some half-filled ones |
| Indicator glyph | The U gate's two indicator functions used `\mathbb 1`, which the AMS blackboard font cannot print; both rendered as a wrong symbol, in the published v2.6 PDF too. `dsfont` is loaded and `\mathds{1}` prints the intended glyph |
| Fonts | All embedded in both builds (the published v2.6 PDF has one unembedded Helvetica) |
| Table of contents | All 62 entries checked against the page each heading actually starts on: all correct |
| Links | 310 internal cross-references, all resolving; 0 external links in the anonymous build (the source had one, the correspondence mailto) |
| Text outside the type block | 0 blocks |

**Analysis.**
1. **The figure repair is the one that mattered.** A stale equation number inside an image survives every LaTeX check — the build reports no warning, because from LaTeX's view there is no reference at all. It was found by reading the figure, not the log, and it was wrong in the published v2.6 PDF too.
2. **`paper-sources/figures/make_figures.py` is not modified.** The repair script patches a copy in a temporary directory and runs that, so the delivered source stays as the owner sent it.
3. **The remaining float gaps are not worth chasing yet.** Every approved wording change will reflow the document; tightening placement now would have to be redone. Revisit once the text stops changing (DK-09).

**"Without automatic cuts" holds.** Comparing the source build with the TMLR edition item by item:

| | Source build | TMLR edition | Lost | Added |

| Figures | 2 | 2 | none | none |

The 28-word difference is the title block: the centred authors, affiliation, correspondence line and date that `\maketitle` replaces, against the anonymous banner it adds. No section, table, figure or equation is missing.

**The template is the current official one.** `tmlr.sty`, `tmlr.bst` and `fancyhdr.sty` are copied at build time from a clone of `JmlrOrg/tmlr-style-file` at commit `7bf90ef` (2023-06-30) — and that is still the newest commit upstream, confirmed against the repository's commit list. Note that these files are **not committed** to this PR, so the provenance rests on the clone rather than on anything reviewable here; a reviewer wanting to check it must clone the same repository.

**Decision B4 was right and two other records were wrong.** B4 states the title-page date is not carried into the TMLR edition. That is correct — the string appears **nowhere** in either edition, 0 occurrences in the whole document. DK-02 and DK-11 had both asserted the candidate's title page still shows it, from reading `main.tex` rather than the PDF. Both are corrected.

## Not done / limitations

- The approved wording changes (DK-01 A8, DK-04 items, DK-06 P1–P8) are **not applied**; they wait for the owner and will be made in one pass.
- The author record is DK-02's, and the converter now **reads it** rather than restating it: `--author-record <author_record.json>` builds the TMLR `\author` block from that one structure. Without the flag the built-in placeholder is used **and a warning is printed**, because that placeholder was wrong — it put Dingyi Kang at the wrong affiliation with no email, and since the conversion replaces the source's own title block, the wrong version is what rendered regardless of what the source said. A build can no longer print an unconfirmed author list silently. The contribution statement in the manuscript is still untouched and still names two authors.
- Float gaps and the three interior pages with ~100pt of white space were left for DK-09, and **DK-09 has now measured them**: four interior gaps over 55pt across pages 42, 48 and 59, the largest 86pt, all beside large tables. Ordinary float spacing, not a layout failure.
