== conversion (tmlr-prep/dk03/convert_to_tmlr.py)
  dropped \pdfoutput=1 (arXiv-specific; OpenReview takes the PDF as built)
  dropped \documentclass (the wrapper supplies it, at 10pt as TMLR expects)
  dropped \usepackage{inputenc} - tmlr.sty sets T1 fontenc and lmodern; utf8 is the modern default
  dropped \usepackage{fontenc} - tmlr.sty loads [T1]{fontenc}
  dropped \usepackage{geometry} - tmlr.sty sets the TMLR page geometry
  dropped \usepackage{mathptmx} - TMLR uses its own (Computer Modern / lmodern) fonts
  dropped \usepackage{helvet} - same
  dropped \usepackage{courier} - same
  dropped \usepackage{natbib} - tmlr.sty loads natbib itself; a second load clashes on options
  dropped \usepackage{fancyhdr} - tmlr.sty loads fancyhdr and sets the TMLR running head
  pdfauthor now expands \pdfauthorlist, which each wrapper defines (empty for the anonymous build) - otherwise the metadata leaks the authors
  dropped the source's fancyhdr running-head block (TMLR sets its own head)
  layout: retuned the float placement parameters (topfraction, textfraction, floatpagefraction, totalnumber) and confined floats to their own section with placeins, at section, subsection and subsubsection level - the source's settings leave table-only pages in the narrower TMLR block, and let tables drift above their own heading
  replaced the centred title block with TMLR \title/\author/\maketitle
  bibliography style plainnat -> tmlr (TMLR's author-year style)
  layout: the U-gate display overflowed the TMLR text block by 14.9pt; set on two aligned lines instead of one
  layout: loaded dsfont, which supplies a real indicator glyph (see the next fix)
  layout: the two indicator functions in the U gate used \mathbb 1; the AMS blackboard font has no digits, so both printed as a wrong symbol in the published v2.6 PDF as well. \mathds{1} prints the intended indicator
  copied the TMLR style files, references.bib and both figures into dk03_logonly/

== changes recorded after later review passes
  the title-page date (September 3, 2026) is not carried into the TMLR edition: it lived in the centred
    block that \maketitle replaces, and the template has no date field for submissions. Decision B4.
  the correspondence mailto link went with that block; the anonymous build has no external links at all.
  the U-gate indicator functions used \mathbb 1. The AMS blackboard font has no digits, so both printed
    as a wrong symbol - in the published v2.6 PDF as well. dsfont is loaded and \mathds{1} prints the
    intended indicator. This corrects a defect of the published paper, not of the conversion.
  floats were confined to their own section, subsection and subsubsection (placeins plus two barriers).
    Without it, tables drifted above their own heading: 6.1, 11.2 and 15.2 each showed a heading with the
    next heading directly under it, their table having floated onto an earlier page.
